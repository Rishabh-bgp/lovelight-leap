Once upon a sunset…

Amara and Theo promised to meet at the Moonlit Castle when the stars came out. But a gloomy storm has made the whole kingdom grumpy, and the path is full of sulky creatures.

Leap through the Rose Garden, the Candy-Cloud Sky and the Starry Night City, cheer up everyone you meet with a hop or a heart arrow, and find the love letters Theo left along the way.

How to play

Controls
Move← → or A D
JumpSpace, Z, ↑ or W (hold to jump higher)
Heart arrowX (hold ↑ to aim up)
Drop down↓ + jump on a thin ledge
PauseP or Esc
SoundM mutes everything
  • Hearts add to your score. Every 100 gives an extra life.
  • Find the 3 love letters hidden in each world.
  • Hop on grumpy creatures to cheer them up.
  • Cupid’s Bow shoots heart arrows. The Sparkle Shield keeps you safe for 8 seconds.
  • Light a heart lantern to save your place.
  • Earn up to 3 stars: finish, find every letter, and beat the par time.
  • A gamepad works too, and phones get touch buttons.
